# From the Source

**From the Source** is a food and recipe platform built for people who love discovering, recreating, and reverse-engineering their favorite restaurant dishes.

Users can post bounties for specific restaurant recipes, hunt down and submit recreations, and verify accuracy through taste tests or direct confirmation from restaurants.

---

## Concept

Have you ever wanted to recreate *that one sauce* or *that one dish* from a restaurant?

From the Source turns that curiosity into a game:
- Users post recipe bounties with point rewards
- Hunters claim bounties and attempt to crack the recipe
- Submissions are verified by the community (or the restaurant itself)
- The best hunters rise to the top of the leaderboard

---

## Core Features

- **MUSIC**: THE WEBPAGE WILL PLAY FROM THE START BY LAUFEY WHEN IT OPENS (please email mhhan@mit.edu "haha" to let her know u get the pun)

- **Recipe Bounties**: Users post requests for specific restaurant recipes with point rewards and time limits.

- **Bounty Hunting**: Hunters claim bounties and submit recipes with a required Quest Log explaining how the recipe was obtained.

- **Verification**: Recipes are verified through community taste tests (1–10 accuracy ratings) or restaurant confirmation.

- **Restaurant Profiles**: Each restaurant has a page showcasing cracked recipes, verification rates, and active bounties.

- **Difficulty Tags**: Recipes are labeled by how they were obtained (e.g. walked in and asked, reverse-engineered, multiple attempts).

- **Points & Leaderboards**: Users earn points for contributing and verifying recipes, with leaderboards highlighting top contributors.

- **Search & Filters**: Recipes can be searched and filtered by cuisine, location, difficulty, verification status, and bounty size.
